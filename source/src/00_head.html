    Trinetra AR

    Walk a field compound wearing a see-through helmet display. Everything from the build spec runs live, so you can learn the HUD before you solder anything.

    • 3D compass ring
    • Heading-up minimap
    • Drone day / IR feed
    • Rearview mirror
    • 5 vision modes
    • Radial menu
    • Teammates through walls
    • Team pings
    • Alert queue
    • Exploded helmet
    • Stereo 3D view
    • Glasses field of view
    Loading sky, terrain and teammates… 0%

    Use headphones at low volume; alert tones play without warning. Teammates, the drone and every reading are simulated. The simulator has no weapons, no opponents and no targeting.

    Night sky: Poly Haven (CC0). Grass texture: OpenGameArt (CC-BY 3.0). Teammate character: Mixamo, from the three.js examples (recoloured). Day sky: Preetham sky model. 3D engine: three.js (MIT).
